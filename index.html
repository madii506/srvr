01 — how it works 01 / 04

a coin, a server, and a door only holders can open.

  1. launch

    name it, draw it, point it at your server. it goes live on pump.fun from your own wallet.

  2. lock the rent

    the coin's creator fees are routed to the server's wallet at launch, and the split is locked on chain. nobody can change it after, not even you.

  3. hold to join

    holders sign with their wallet and add their game name. the server pulls its whitelist from srvr every two minutes.

  4. sell, and the door shuts

    every sync reads the chain again. wallets that sold drop off the list on their own.

    07 — faq
    
      
    questions.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    what is srvr?
    a launchpad on pump.fun where every coin comes with its own game server. the coin's creator fees pay the server's rent, and holding the coin is the key to get in.
    
        
    does srvr host my server?
    no. you run your server wherever you like, on any host or your own machine. srvr launches the coin, locks where its fees go, and keeps your whitelist in sync with who holds.
    
        
    where do the fees go?
    all of the coin's pump.fun creator fees go to the rent wallet you set at launch. the split is written on chain with pump.fun's fee sharing and its admin is revoked in the same launch, so it can't be changed later. anyone can push waiting fees to the rent wallet with "pay the rent" on the coin's page.
    
        
    does srvr take a cut?
    no. srvr adds no fee to launches, trades or joins. pump.fun charges its usual fees.
    
        
    who can join a server?
    anyone who holds the coin and links a wallet and a game name. joining is free: you sign a message, not a transaction.
    
        
    what happens if i sell?
    the next sync reads the chain again. if your wallet no longer holds the coin, you drop off the whitelist, and you're kicked if you're online and the server has that switched on.
    
        
    is the plugin safe to run?
    it reads one address (your coin's whitelist feed) and adds or removes whitelist entries. nothing else. the source is public, so read it before you install it.
    
        
    which servers does it work with?
    java edition servers running paper, spigot or purpur 1.13 or newer. any server can also pull the feed with cron. ping works on any java edition server.
    
        
    is srvr affiliated with mojang, microsoft or pump.fun?
    no. srvr is independent. servers that charge for access or perks should follow mojang's usage guidelines.
